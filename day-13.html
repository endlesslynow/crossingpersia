Day 13 · 29 November, 500 BCE

Haliartos to Koroneia

◆ ◆ Map

The sky had cleared by morning, and I left to the town of Koroneia in thin sunshine that did nothing to dry the muddy road. A few kilometres west I stopped at the spring Tilphossa, beside a small temple of Apollo. The spring water came up cold and clear, and there was room around it for a whole crowd of travellers to rest among the surrounding trees.

After a rest at the spring, I walked on a strip of land between the lake and the mountain. Every bit of it was ploughed. The earth there was black and heavy, the richest earth I've seen since I left Athens. Farmers were already out ploughing again after the rain, with mud up to their knees. Polykritos had told me that Koroneia always fought beside Thebes, so I wondered how an Athenian would be greeted there.

It was early afternoon when I reached Koroneia. There, I met a man named Hermaios at the edge of the town, bringing his horses in from the fields. Apparently he was a horse breeder. He offered to give me a bed and a seat by his hearth for a few Theban coins. The hearth was warm but the looks his daughter Thaleia gave me were even warmer. I'm only two weeks into my journey and already tempted to abandon my plans for the more settled life of marriage and family. From Hermaios' door, which faced towards a lake, I could see the temple of Athena Itonia. Thaleia told me that Athena Itonia was the goddess of Koroneia, and like her, stood forever alone in the fields of Koroneia.

Based on evidence: The sacred spring Tilphossa lay a few kilometres west of Haliartos, near a temple of Apollo Tilphossios. The Hymn to Apollo called it a "lovely place" with "fair water", among groves, wide enough for groups of travellers to rest. Most of the farmland of the Copais basin lay on its west and south shores, and its soils are very fertile. Herodotus quoted the Thebans saying that the Coronaeans "always fight together with us". Pausanias and Strabo put the temple of Athena Itonia in the plain in front of Koroneia, though its exact place is debated.
